OUR COMMITMENT TO YOU

Transparency & Service Level Agreement

Medical billing should never be a mystery. With us, you will always see your money clearly. We hold ourselves to strict timelines and absolute transparency.

  • Claims submitted: Within 24 hours of receipt of complete notes.
  • Rejections actioned: Within 30 days.
  • Patient queries answered: Within 24 working hours.
  • Monthly reports delivered: By the 7th working day of the following month.

Working Hours

  • Monday to Friday: 9:00 - 17:00
  • Saturday, Sunday & Public Holidays: Closed

Transparency For Patients

Your patients will receive billing that is easy to understand and fair.

1. Upfront Estimates

Before treatment, where possible, patients receive a written estimate showing:

  • Treatment planned
  • Expected medical aid payment
  • Expected patient co-payment

No surprise bills.

2. Clear Patient Statements

Patient statements show:

  • Date of treatment
  • Treatment description and codes
  • Amount billed
  • Amount paid by medical aid
  • Amount owed by patient - and why

We use plain language, not just codes.

3. Professional Query Handling

Patient billing queries are answered within 24 working hours, with a full explanation and supporting documents where needed.

Our Transparency Rules

We commit to the following non-negotiable rules:

Rule Our Commitment
Rule 1 Fee is calculated on collected amounts only, and shown in full.
Rule 2 Monthly reports delivered on the same date every month.
Rule 3 No write-offs without provider written approval.
Rule 4 Every claim, payment, and adjustment is documented.
Rule 5 Provider and patient information is kept confidential under POPIA.
Rule 6 Rejections are actioned within 48 hours and reported monthly.

Why Transparency Matters For Your Practice

  • Faster Payment: Clear claims and fast follow-up means you get paid sooner.
  • Lower Bad Debt: Age analysis and collections reduce outstanding balances.
  • Fewer Disputes: Patients who understand their bill complain less.
  • More Trust: Providers who can see their money trust their billing partner.
  • Better Control: You can make decisions based on accurate numbers.
